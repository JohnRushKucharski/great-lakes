# data

Everything in `data/` is tracked, because the workflows read it. Raw inputs are never
edited; the cleaned and completed data products are regenerated by the
[data-product workflows](../workflows/analysis/data-products/avg/README.md) (see
[`docs/data_pipelines.md`](../docs/data_pipelines.md) for the order).

**Units and datum.** All source and data-product values are **meters, IGLD85**. Only
meters/feet and IGLD85/NAVD88 are supported. Conversion to feet and/or NAVD88 is done by the
scripts that make figures and results (`great_lakes.units`), never in the data files. The
summary figures use the defaults, **ft, NAVD88** (about +0.44 ft versus IGLD85), and label
both.

**avg vs swl.** The two datasets are never mixed. **avg** is average (monthly) lake level.
**swl** is storm water level (storm surge and wave set-up, as ARI frequency curves).
USACE sources often call swl "still water level"; it is not the average level. See
[`GLOSSARY.md`](../GLOSSARY.md).

## avg/

`<lake>_avg.csv` for `superior`, `michiganhuron`, `stclair`, `erie`, `ontario`: the cleaned
monthly series (about 12,360 rows, dates to year 2999), one column per scenario. Michigan
and Huron are one hydraulically connected lake, so one file serves both. Produced by
[`workflows/analysis/data-products/avg`](../workflows/analysis/data-products/avg/README.md).

- **Includes:** average monthly level for each of the 17 scenarios.
- **Does not include:** storm surge, wave set-up or any near-shore process.
- `figures/<lake>.png`: mean level surface per lake (5 figures).

## swl/

Workbooks `<lake>_swl.xlsx` for `superior`, `michigan`, `huron`, `ontario` (no St. Clair or
Erie). One sheet per scenario; one row per save point with its water level at each ARI.
Produced by [`workflows/analysis/data-products/swl`](../workflows/analysis/data-products/swl/README.md).

- **Includes:** storm surge and wave set-up, as frequency (ARI) curves per save point.
- **Does not include:** near-shore run-up or overtopping. It is not the average level.
- `clean/`: the 5 known scenarios per lake. Three Michigan save points with no values and
  implausible coordinates are dropped.
- `complete/`: all 17 scenarios. 7 are filled (Delaunay interpolation) and 5 extrapolated
  (row shift) from the 5 known; estimated scenarios have lower confidence
  (see [`scenario_completion.md`](../workflows/analysis/data-products/swl/methods/scenario_completion.md)).
- `figures/<lake>.png` in each folder: a 2x2 of ARI 1, 10, 100 and 1000 with one colorbar
  per panel, at the save point nearest the lake centroid; in `complete/`, estimated cells
  carry a small dot.
